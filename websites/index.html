Sheet W3 · How it works

From first email to launch.

You don't need to know anything about websites. You know your business; I'll handle the rest and keep you in the loop.

  1. Say hello. Email me about your business. We'll talk for about 15 minutes, by phone or email, about what you need. It's free, and there's no obligation.
  2. Get a plan. I look at what you have now, if anything, and send you a short plan: what the site will include, what it will say, and what I need from you. Point me to anything you like, including the sites below or other businesses' sites, and I'll use it as a starting point.
  3. Kick off. Send me your logo, photos, hours, and services. Don't have good photos? I'll help you pick from what's on your phone. There's nothing to pay yet.
  4. Build and review. Most sites are ready for your review within a few days. I send you a private link. You tell me what to change, and I fix it until you're happy with it.
  5. Approve and launch. When you're happy with the site, you pay the $250 build fee and pick a monthly plan. Then I connect your domain, put the site online, and set up the basics so Google can find you.
  6. Stay up to date. Your plan covers hosting and security. With a maintenance plan, just email me (or call, on Advanced) when something changes.

Before we start

What I'll ask you for

  • Your business name, phone number, address, and hours
  • What you offer, in your own words
  • Your logo, if you have one
  • A few photos of your work, shop, or team
  • Your domain name, if you already own one

Sheet W6 · Questions

Good questions.

Who owns my domain?

You do. Your domain (like yourbusiness.com) is registered in your name, usually for about $10 to $20 a year paid to the registrar. If you already have one, I'll connect it. If not, I'll walk you through buying it.

What counts as an update?

Small changes: new hours or prices, swapping photos, adding a review, posting a seasonal special, or fixing a phone number. Most of these take me a few minutes each, so 15 minutes covers several of them. New pages or a redesign are bigger jobs, and I'll quote those separately before starting.

What if I don't use my monthly time?

It doesn't carry over to the next month. If you rarely need changes, the $9 Hosting plan is probably the better fit, and you can switch anytime.

Can I switch plans or cancel?

Yes, anytime. Email me and I'll change it. If you ever leave, I'll send you your site's files (the pages, images, and code) for free so you can host it somewhere else. Sites with extras like logins or a client portal also depend on outside services that don't come with the files.

Will I show up on Google?

I set up the basics search engines need and help you set up your free Google Business Profile, which is what shows up in Google Maps. Nobody can honestly promise you the top spot, but these steps are what most small businesses are missing.

Do you only work with rural businesses?

Small-town and rural businesses are who I built this for, but if you're somewhere else and this sounds right, email me anyway.
